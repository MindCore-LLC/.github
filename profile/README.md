## About Affective

Human communication is more than words.

**Affective LLC** is an AI research and development company working to make artificial intelligence more emotionally aware, context-sensitive, and natural to interact with.

We believe the next step for AI isn't simply generating better answers. It's building systems that can better understand the people they're communicating with, recognize when context changes, and respond with greater care and nuance.

## What we're working toward

- **Emotional understanding.** AI that pays attention to how people express themselves, not just what they type.
- **Adaptive interactions.** Experiences that become more relevant and responsive as context evolves.
- **Natural communication.** More fluid, expressive interactions across text and voice.
- **Responsible design.** Privacy, user control, and measurable progress as first-class priorities.

## Our work on GitHub

This organization is home to selected Affective projects, tools, and engineering work as they become ready to share. Our core research and systems are still in development.

We're building toward a future where technology understands people better, and people don't have to work so hard to be understood.
